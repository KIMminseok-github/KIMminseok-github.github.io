Minseok Kim, Ph.D.

Senior Research Engineer, Data Intelligence Team, Data Science Division, TelePIX

I develop satellite retrieval algorithms that combine radiative transfer physics with artificial intelligence. My work centers on the geostationary air-quality constellation (GEMS, TEMPO, Sentinel-4) and its companion imagers (AMI, ABI, FCI), and on bringing these algorithms from research into operational and onboard use.

Email ORCID GitHub

Research

Multi-sensor fusion

Combining spectrometers and imagers on geostationary platforms to obtain information no single instrument provides: fused aerosol optical depth, stereoscopic aerosol height, and UV–visible spectral absorption.

Physics-constrained AI

Deep-learning radiative transfer emulators and hybrid retrievals that keep the physics explicit while removing computational bottlenecks — AI for what it learns well, physics for the rest.

Operational and onboard processing

Taking algorithms to production: operational geostationary aerosol products, and lightweight atmospheric algorithms designed for onboard processors on small satellites.

Research topics

Geostationary aerosol retrieval and fusion

  • Fusion of aerosol optical depth from GEMS, AMI, and GOCI-II using statistical and deep-learning methods.
  • Geometric stereoscopic aerosol top height from two geostationary imagers.
  • Aerosol fine-mode fraction from AMI with deep learning and spectral deconvolution.
  • Operational update of the GEMS Level-2 aerosol algorithm (with the National Institute of Environmental Research, 2024–2025).

Physics–AI hybrid retrieval

  • Hybrid aerosol retrieval for GK-2A/AMI using a deep-learning radiative transfer emulator in place of online VLIDORT calculations.
  • UV–visible spectral aerosol absorption from GEMS and AMI, combining optimal estimation with deep-learning emulation.
  • Neural emulators of top-of-atmosphere reflectance for UV–NIR bands.

Greenhouse gases

  • Methane plume detection from MTG-FCI with AI automation, in preparation for GEO-KOMPSAT-5.

High-resolution and onboard processing

  • Atmospheric correction for high-resolution small satellites without SWIR bands, with joint aerosol–surface retrieval.
  • Satellite-based estimation of surface PM2.5 from high-resolution imagery.
  • Change detection from sub-meter imagery for land monitoring.
  • Onboard versions of PM2.5, dehazing, and atmospheric correction algorithms for the TelePIX TetraPLEX processor; ground-validated, with hardware testing and in-orbit demonstration planned.
